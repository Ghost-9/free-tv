Free TV · sound lab

Ten UI sounds, made for the TV.

Short, low-volume, no high-frequency bite. 48 kHz mono; files in app/assets/sounds/. Recommended pairing: Tick Soft for focus, Glass Ping for open.

Peak-normalised to −12 dBFS; on the TV they will feel quieter than on a phone. Click Play to audition.
