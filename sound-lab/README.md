# ArtCraft sound lab

Ten UI sounds, generated locally for the TV (48 kHz, mono, 16-bit WAV, peak-normalised to −12 dBFS).
No sampled material; the files are CC0.

Open `index.html` in a browser and click **Play** to audition. The app assets are copied to
`app/assets/sounds/`; the chosen one will be wired in `P1` behind a **Sound** setting, off by default.

| # | File | Use | Length | Character |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `01-tick-soft.wav` | focus | 45 ms | almost subliminal |
| 2 | `02-tock-low.wav` | focus | 60 ms | warm, muted |
| 3 | `03-wood-tap.wav` | focus | 55 ms | dry acoustic |
| 4 | `04-glass-ping.wav` | open | 160 ms | bright, short |
| 5 | `05-marimba.wav` | confirm | 220 ms | musical |
| 6 | `06-water-drop.wav` | open | 140 ms | soft sweep |
| 7 | `07-soft-pop.wav` | focus | 45 ms | round, quiet |
| 8 | `08-paper-tick.wav` | focus | 40 ms | textural |
| 9 | `09-double-tick.wav` | confirm | 100 ms | two-step |
| 10 | `10-chime.wav` | open | 340 ms | longest |

Recommended pairing: **1** for focus, **4** for open. On the TV they will feel quieter than on a phone;
the TV's own volume applies.
